Last Updated October 14th, 2026

No Subscriptions

Runwae has no subscription plans and no recurring charges. A marketer pays once for each gig, at the moment they choose to pay for it. Runwae does not keep your card on file, so it cannot charge you again unless you pay for another gig. There is no plan to cancel and no renewal to stop.

Stop Using Runwae at Any Time

You may stop using Runwae at any time, for any reason. To close your account, use Remove Account in Account settings (you confirm with your password), or email hello@runwae.com from the address on your account and we will close it for you.

When your account is closed, your sign-in is deleted, your email address is removed from your profile, and your posts are marked inactive. Our Privacy Policy (section “Data Retention”) explains what we keep afterwards and how to ask us to delete it.

Influencers and Stripe

Influencers receive payments in their own Stripe account. Payouts from Stripe to your bank are handled by Stripe under its own terms. If you want your Stripe account disconnected from Runwae, email hello@runwae.com.

Gigs in Progress

Closing your account does not reverse a payment that has already been made. A marketer payment for a gig follows the refund rule in our Refund Policy and the section “Monetary Refunds (Marketer)” of the Terms and Conditions: a refund can be requested within 14 days of payment while the influencer has not yet accepted the payment, and none is issued after the influencer accepts it. If you have a gig in progress, contact us at hello@runwae.com before you close your account.

Contact

If you have questions about closing your account, contact us at hello@runwae.com.
